* gcData/posf10264_init.csv
* gcData/posf10289_init.csv
* gcData/posf10325_init.csv
* gcData/posf11498_init.csv

## HEFA:Jet-A Blends
GCxGC data for HEFA fuels produced from diﬀerent feedstocks (camelina, tallow, and mixed fat) courtesy of [Vozka et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.energyfuels.8b02787). Note that these can be blended with Jet-A to reproduce the density and viscosity measurement data from that paper:
* gcData/hefa-came.csv
* gcData/hefa-tall.csv
* gcData/hefa-mfat.csv
* gcData/jet-a.csv

# UNIFAC Subgroup Decomposition Data

The `unifacDecomposition/<name>.csv` files supply the per-compound UNIFAC subgroup
counts used by `fuel.activity()` and `fuel.mixture_vapor_pressure(..., activity_model="UNIFAC")`.

Format:
- One row per compound, matching the row order and count in `gcData/<name>_init.csv`.
- 113 subgroup-count columns, in the order they appear in `unifacTableData/unifac_subgroups.csv`.
- First column `Compound` matches the corresponding `Compound` entry in `gcData/<name>_init.csv`.

Convention (classical UNIFAC, Magnussen 1981):
- `ACCH3`, `ACCH2`, `ACCH` are **2-atom subgroups** — they include the aromatic
  ring carbon AND the directly-bonded aliphatic carbon together. Their R/Q values
  equal AC + CH3/CH2/CH.
- `AC` is used only for aromatic ring carbons that have **no H and no aliphatic
  substituent** (typically ring-fusion junction carbons like the 4a, 8a positions
  of naphthalene).
- `ACH` is each aromatic ring carbon carrying one H.
- This differs from the convention used by the Constantinou-Gani group
  decomposition files in `groupDecompositionData/`, where ACCH₂/ACCH₃ encode
  only the aliphatic atom and the aromatic ring carbon is counted separately
  as AC.

All 13 fuel decompositions were generated by `tools/build_unifac_decompositions.py`
from a hand-curated name→SMILES table and the RDKit-based classifier in
`tools/decompose_unifac.py`. Each compound row is cross-checked against the
molecular formula derived from its SMILES; mismatches abort the build.

* propertiesData/decane-NIST.csv
* propertiesData/dodecane-NIST.csv

The properties data for POSF fuels is from the AFRL [Edwards (2017)](https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2017-0146) and [Edwards (2020)](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/AD1093317.pdf):
* propertiesData/posf10264.csv
* propertiesData/posf10289.csv
* propertiesData/posf10325.csv
* propertiesData/posf11498.csv
